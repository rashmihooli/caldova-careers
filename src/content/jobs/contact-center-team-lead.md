You'll coach and support a team of contact center representatives, making sure every caller gets accurate, empathetic help while keeping the operation running smoothly.

- Monitor quality and turnaround, and improve response playbooks
- Partner with sales enablement to keep product knowledge current

## Qualifications

- Experience leading a contact center or customer support team
- Strong communication and coaching skills
- Comfort using data to spot and fix bottlenecks
