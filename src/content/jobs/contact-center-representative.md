## About the role

You'll answer questions, resolve issues, and route requests to the right teams — making sure everyone who contacts Caldova has a clear, helpful experience.

## Responsibilities

- Respond to inbound questions from clinicians, patients, and partners
- Log and route requests accurately, following established playbooks
- Escalate complex issues while keeping the caller informed

## Qualifications

- Customer service or contact center experience
- Clear, empathetic written and verbal communication
- Comfort learning product details and using support tools
